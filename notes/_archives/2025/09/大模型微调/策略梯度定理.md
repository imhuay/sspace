## 背景
> [_策略优化_](./强化学习基础_RLHF.md#策略优化)

- 强化学习的目标是学习一个能够 **最大化期望回报** 的 **策略**, 这一过程被称为 **策略优化 (Policy Optimization)**.
- 由于期望回报关于策略参数的梯度通常无法直接求解, 所以一般采用 **梯度上升** 等迭代方法来优化策略;
- **策略梯度定理**:
    <!-- $$\boxed{
        \nabla_\theta J(\theta)
        =\mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot G_t \ \right\rbrack
        ,\quad G_t=\sum_{k=t}^{\infty} \gamma^{k}R_{k+1}
    }$$ -->
    - **目标函数 (期望回报) $J(\theta)$ 关于策略参数 $\theta$ 的梯度, 可以表示为关于轨迹分布的期望**;
    - **这使我们可以通过对 <u>轨迹采样</u> 来对梯度进行无偏估计**.

## 推导
> 以下推导旨在求得期望回报 $J(\theta)$ 关于策略参数 $\theta$ 梯度的具体期望表达式;

> 🚨**以下推导统一使用 *相对折扣记法***, 即 $\displaystyle G_t = \sum_{k=t}^{\infty}\gamma^{k-t}R_{k+1} = \sum_{k=t}^{\infty}\gamma^{k-t}r_{k+1}(s_t, a_t)$.
>> 注: ***绝对折扣记法*** $\displaystyle \tilde{G}_{t} = \sum_{k=t}^{\infty}\gamma^{k}R_{k+1} = \gamma^t G_t$, 两者差了一个 $\gamma^t$ 因子; <br>
>> 物理含义: 相对记法 **以当前时刻为基准进行折扣**, 绝对记法 **以轨迹起点为基准进行折扣**.

### 基本形式
> 也称为 **REINFORCE 策略梯度公式**

> **推导目标**: 
> - 从目标函数出发:
>   $$\begin{align}\boxed{
>       J(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim p_\theta(\tau)} \big\lbrack G(\tau)\big\rbrack ,\quad G(\tau) = G_0
>   }\end{align}$$
> - 得到策略梯度的基本形式:
>   $$\begin{align}\boxed{
>   \nabla_\theta J(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \underline{\gamma^t G_t} \ \right\rbrack
>   }\end{align}$$
>> [注](#注-基本形式的另一种表示)

---
> #### 注: 基本形式的另一种表示
> $$\begin{align}
> \nabla_\theta J(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \underline{G_t} \ \right\rbrack
> \end{align}$$
> 我认为的两种解释: <br>
> 1. 这里的 $G_t$ 实际上是 **绝对折扣回报**, 即 $\displaystyle \tilde{G}_t = \sum_{k=t}^{\infty}\gamma^{k}R_{k+1}$, 与相对折扣回报正好差一个 $\gamma^t$ 因子;
> 2. 在实际优化中, 系数 $\gamma^t$ 的 **平均缩放效应** 通过调整 **学习率** 做了部分补偿, 因此被省略了.
---

- 对 $J(\theta)$ 求梯度:
    $$\begin{align}
        \nabla_\theta J(\theta) = \nabla_\theta \mathbb{E}_{\tau \sim p_{\theta}(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ G(\tau) \ \right\rbrack 
        &= \nabla_\theta \int_{\mathcal{T}} p_\theta(\tau) \underline{G(\tau)} d\tau \\
        &= \int_{\mathcal{T}} \nabla_\theta\ p_\theta(\tau) \underline{G(\tau)} d\tau
    \end{align}$$
    > ◦ **积分域 (轨迹空间 $\mathcal{T}$) 与参数无关**: 策略只影响轨迹出现的概率分布, 而不会改变轨迹集合本身; <br>
    > ◦ 同时其他正则条件默认成立: **策略关于参数可微, 且积分收敛良好**; <br>
    > 因此可以安全地 **交换积分与梯度**. <br>
- 其中轨迹分布: 
    $$\begin{align}
        p_\theta(\tau) = p(s_0)\prod_{t=0}^{\infty}\pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\ P(s_{t+1}|s_t,a_t)
    \end{align}$$
- 根据 **对数微分性质**:
    $$\begin{align}
        \nabla_\theta p_\theta(\tau) = p_\theta(\tau) \frac{\nabla_\theta p_\theta(\tau)}{p_\theta(\tau)} = p_\theta(\tau) \nabla_\theta \log p_\theta(\tau)
    \end{align}$$
- 代回, 得:
    $$\begin{align}
        \nabla_\theta J(\theta) = \int_{\mathcal{T}} p_\theta(\tau) \underline{\nabla_\theta \log p_\theta(\tau) G(\tau)} d\tau = \mathbb{E}_{\tau \sim p_{\theta}(\tau)} \big\lbrack \nabla_\theta \log p_\theta(\tau) G(\tau) \big\rbrack
    \end{align}$$
    > 该公式是 [Score Function 恒等式](#score-function-恒等式) 的重要推论.
- 展开 $\nabla_\theta \log p_\theta(\tau)$:
    $$\begin{align}
      \nabla_\theta \log p_\theta(\tau) 
      &= \nabla_\theta \log \left\lbrack p(s_0)\prod_{t=0}^{\infty} \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\ P(s_{t+1}|s_t,a_t) \right\rbrack \\
      &= \nabla_\theta \left\lbrack \log p(s_0)+\sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)+\sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\log P(s_{t+1}|s_t,a_t) \right\rbrack  \\
        &= \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \nabla_\theta \log \pi_\theta(a_t | s_t)
    \end{align}$$
    > ◦ 初始项与环境项不含参数 $\theta$, 因此梯度为零; <br>
    > ◦ 这表明我们 **不需要知道环境的转移概率 $P(s_{t+1}|s_t,a_t)$, 只需采样轨迹即可估计梯度**. <br>
- 代回, 得:
    $$\begin{align}
        \nabla_\theta J(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{\tau \sim p_{\theta}(\tau)} \left\lbrack \left( \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \nabla_\theta \log \pi_\theta(a_t | s_t) \right) \cdot G(\tau) \right\rbrack
    \end{align}$$
- 根据 [**基线不变性**](#基线不变性), 可证:
    $$\begin{align}
        \mathbb{E}_{\tau \sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \nabla_\theta \log \pi_\theta(a_t | s_t) \big(G(\tau) - \gamma^t G_t\big) \right\rbrack = 0
    \end{align}$$
- 因此:
    $$\begin{align}
        \nabla_\theta J(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \underline{\gamma^t G_t} \ \right\rbrack
    \end{align}$$
    > 因为 $G_t$ 依然是从分布 $\tau\sim p_\theta(\tau)$ 中采样获得, 所以不需要修改期望下标.
- **推导完毕**.

#### 基本形式的直观理解
- $\nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)$ 只影响 **未来** 的动作选择;
- 在给定历史的条件下, **过去的回报** 与 **未来的动作概率** $\nabla_\theta \log \pi_\theta(a_t | s_t)$ 是统计独立的;
    - 因此 **过去的回报** 不能为如何调整未来动作的概率提供有效的梯度信号.
- 这个性质确保了策略梯度只依赖于动作的 **未来后果**.

### Q 函数形式
> 推导目标:
> - 从 **轨迹视角** 的基本形式出发:
>   $$\begin{align}\boxed{
>   \nabla_\theta J(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{\tau \sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \underline{\gamma^t G_t} \ \right\rbrack
>   }\end{align}$$
> - 到 **状态-动作视角** 的 Q 函数形式:
>   $$\begin{align}\boxed{
>   \nabla_\theta J(\theta) = \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \mathbb{E}_{\underline{s_t,a_t \sim p_t(s_t,a_t)}} \left\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \underline{\gamma^t Q^{\pi_\theta}(s_t, a_t)} \ \right\rbrack
>   }\end{align}$$
>   其中 <br>
>   ◦ $\ p_t(s,a)$ 是轨迹分布 $p_\theta(\tau)$ 关于 **状态-动作对 $(s,a)$** 在时间步 $t$ 的 **边缘分布**. <br>
>   ◦ $\ Q^{\pi_\theta}(s_t, a_t) = \mathbb{E}_{\tau \sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ G_t \ |\ s_t, a_t \ \right\rbrack$. <br>
>   [[注](#注-一种不规范的写法)]
> - 再到 **稳态分布视角** 的 Q 函数形式:
>   $$\begin{align}\boxed{
>   \nabla_\theta J(\theta) \propto \mathbb{E}_{\underline{s \sim d^{\pi_\theta},\ a \sim \pi_\theta(\cdot|s)}} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a|s)\cdot \underline{Q^{\pi_\theta}(s, a)} \ \Big\rbrack
>   }\end{align}$$
>   ◦ 其中 $\displaystyle d^{\pi_\theta}(s) = (1 - \gamma) \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \gamma^t \Pr(s_t = s | \pi_\theta)$ 是 **(归一化) 折扣状态访问分布**. <br>
---
> #### 注: 一种不规范的写法
>> 一开始我把状态-动作视角的 Q 函数形式写作:
>> $$\begin{align}
>> \nabla_\theta J(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{s_t,a_t \sim p_t(s_t,a_t)} \bigg\lbrack\ \underline{\sum_{t=0}^{\infty}} \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \gamma^t Q^{\pi_\theta}(s_t, a_t) \ \bigg\rbrack
>> \end{align}$$
>> 即把 $\sum_{t=0}^{\infty}$ 写在期望内;

>> **这种写法是有问题的**, 因为这里期望下标 $s_t,a_t \sim p_t(s_t,a_t)$ 中的 **边缘分布 $p_t(s_t,a_t)$** 是依赖于时间 $t$ 的.

>> 如果把 $\sum_{t=0}^{\infty}$ 写在期望内, 相当于是说, 你从 $p_t(s_t,a_t)$ 采样了一对 $(s_t, a_t)$, 然后可以从中得到了 $t=0,1,2,...$ 的整条轨迹 $(s_0,a_0,s_1,a_1,...)$, 这显然是不对的.

---

- 根据期望的线性性质, 交换求和与期望, 有:
    $$\begin{align}
        \nabla_\theta J(\theta) 
        &= \mathbb{E}_{\tau \sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \underline{\sum_{t=0}^{\infty}} \ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \gamma^t G_t \ \right\rbrack \\
        &= \underline{\sum_{t=0}^{\infty}} \ \mathbb{E}_{\tau \sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \gamma^t G_t \ \right\rbrack
    \end{align}$$
- 对每个固定的时间步 $t$, 应用 **全期望公式**, 有:
    $$\begin{align}
        & \mathbb{E}_{\tau \sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) \cdot \gamma^t G_t \ \right\rbrack \\
        =\ \ & \mathbb{E}_{s_t,a_t \sim p_t(s_t,a_t)} \Big\lbrack\ \mathbb{E}_{\tau \sim p_\theta(\tau)} \big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \gamma^t G_t \ \big|\ {s_t, a_t} \ \big\rbrack \Big\rbrack
    \end{align}$$
    > 通过这一变换, 我们将原来关于 **轨迹 $\tau$** 的期望, 转化为了关于 **状态-动作对 $(s_t,a_t)$** 在其边缘分布 $p_t(s_t,a_t)$ 下的期望; <br>
- **关键观察**:
    - 在给定 $s_t, a_t$ 条件下, $\boxed{\nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) \cdot \gamma^t}$ 是一个 **确定性常数**.
    - 因此可以将其提出内层期望, 即:
    $$\begin{align}
        \_ 
        &= \mathbb{E}_{s_t,a_t \sim p_t(s_t,a_t)} \Big\lbrack\ \underline{\nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \gamma^t} \ \mathbb{E}_{\tau \sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ G_t \mid {s_t, a_t} \ \right\rbrack \Big\rbrack \\
        &= \mathbb{E}_{s_t,a_t \sim p_t(s_t,a_t)} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \gamma^t \ \underline{Q^{\pi_\theta}(s_t, a_t)} \ \Big\rbrack
    \end{align}$$
    - 代回即证.
- **第一阶段推导完毕**.

---

- 将状态-动作视角的策略梯度展开:
    $$\begin{align}
        \nabla_\theta J(\theta) &= \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \mathbb{E}_{\underline{s_t,a_t \sim p_t(s_t,a_t)}} \left\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \gamma^t Q^{\pi_\theta}(s_t, a_t) \ \right\rbrack \\
        &= \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \sum_{\underline{s_t,a_t}} \ \underline{p_t(s_t, a_t)} \ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \gamma^t Q^{\pi_\theta}(s_t, a_t)
    \end{align}$$
    - 边缘分布 $p_t(s_t, a_t)$ 本身是关于 $s_t$ 和 $a_t$ 的联合分布, 进一步分解:
        $$\begin{align}
            p_t(s_t, a_t) = p_t(s_t) \cdot \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)
        \end{align}$$
    - 代入, 得:
        $$\begin{align}
            \_ &= \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \sum_{s_t,a_t} \underline{p_t(s_t) \cdot \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)} \ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \gamma^t Q^{\pi_\theta}(s_t, a_t) \\
            &= \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \sum_{s_t} p_t(s_t) \sum_{a_t} \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) \ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \gamma^t Q^{\pi_\theta}(s_t, a_t)
        \end{align}$$
- 调整求和顺序, 并提取出 $\gamma^t$, 有:
    $$\begin{align}
        \_ = \sum_{s_t} \ \underline{\sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \gamma^t p_t(s_t)} \sum_{a_t} \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) \ \nabla_\theta \log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) \cdot Q^{\pi_\theta}(s_t, a_t)
    \end{align}$$
    - 注意到:
        $$\begin{align}
            \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \gamma^t p_t(s_t) = \frac{1}{1 - \gamma} d^{\pi_\theta}(s)
        \end{align}$$
    - 代入, 得:
        $$\begin{align}
            \_ &= \frac{1}{1 - \gamma} \underline{\sum_{s} d^{\pi_\theta}(s) \sum_{a} \pi_\theta(a|s)} \ \nabla_\theta \log \pi_\theta(a|s) \cdot Q^{\pi_\theta}(s, a) \\
            &= \frac{1}{1 - \gamma} \mathbb{E}_{\underline{s \sim d^{\pi_\theta},\ a \sim \pi_\theta(\cdot|s)}} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a|s)\cdot \underline{Q^{\pi_\theta}(s, a)} \ \Big\rbrack \\
            &\propto \mathbb{E}_{\underline{s \sim d^{\pi_\theta},\ a \sim \pi_\theta(\cdot|s)}} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a|s)\cdot \underline{Q^{\pi_\theta}(s, a)} \ \Big\rbrack
        \end{align}$$
        > 这里把 **哑变量 $s_t, a_t$** 替换为 $s$ 和 $a$, 是**为了表明此时它们已不再是特定时间步的变量**, 而是对所有可能状态和动作的求和.

        > 从定义来说, $d^{\pi_\theta}(s)$ 是折扣下状态 $s$ 的**稳态分布**, 它已经包含了所有时间步的加权平均, 因此不再依赖 $t$.

        > 常量系数 $\frac{1}{1 - \gamma}$ 一般被吸收到学习率中.
- **第er阶段推导完毕**.

---
- 为了从 **轨迹视角** 转到 **状态-动作视角**, 需要先给出时间步 $t$ 下的 $(s_t, a_t)$ 的 **边缘分布**:
    - 对固定的时间步, 记:
        $$\begin{align}
            f_t(\tau) = \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \gamma^t \ Q^{\pi_\theta}(s_t, a_t)
        \end{align}$$
    - 因为 $f_t(\tau)$ 实际上只依赖于轨迹 $\tau$ 在时间步 $t$ 的分量 $(s_t,s_t)$, 因此:
        $$\begin{align}
            f_t(\tau) = f_t(s_t, a_t)
        \end{align}$$
    - 根据 **边缘分布** 的定义, 有:
        $$\begin{align}
            \mathbb{E}_{\tau} \left\lbrack\ f_t(s_t,a_t) \ \right\rbrack 
            &= \sum_{\tau} p_\theta(\tau) \cdot f_t(s_t,a_t) \\
            &= \sum_{s_0,a_0,\dots,s_t,a_t,\dots} p_\theta(\tau) \cdot f_t(s_t,a_t) \\
            &= \sum_{s_t,a_t} \left\lbrack\ \sum_{s_0,a_0,\dots,s_{t-1},a_{t-1},s_{t+1},a_{t+1},\dots} p_\theta(\tau) \ \right\rbrack f_t(s_t,a_t) \\
            &= \sum_{s_t,a_t} p_t(s_t,a_t) \cdot f_t(s_t,a_t) \\
            &= \mathbb{E}_{s_t,a_t \sim p_t(s_t,a_t)} \left\lbrack\ f_t(s_t,a_t) \ \right\rbrack 
        \end{align}$$
        其中 $\boxed{p_t(s_t,a_t)}$ 即 $(s_t,a_t)$ 的边缘分布.
- 对边缘分布 $p_t(s_t,a_t)$ 进行分解:
    - 因为 $p_t(s_t,a_t)$ 本身是关于 $s_t$ 和 $a_t$ 的 **联合分布**, 因此:
        $$\begin{align}
            p_t(s_t, a_t) = p_t(s_t) \cdot \pi_\theta(a_t \mid s_t)
        \end{align}$$
        其中 $p_t(s_t)$ 表示时间步 $t$ 下状态 $s_t$ 的分布, $\pi_\theta(a_t \mid s_t)$;

- 因为每个时间步下格式相同, 以下先对固定时刻 $t$ 进行分析:
    $$\mathbb{E}_{\tau} \left\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \gamma^t G_t \ \right\rbrack$$
- **全期望公式展开**:
    - **对轨迹分解**:
        $$\tau = (\tau_{\le t}, \tau_{> t})$$
        其中: 
        - $\tau_{\le t} = (s_0,a_0,\dots,s_{t-1},a_{t-1},s_t,a_t)$ : 到时刻 $t$ 的历史轨迹, **含 $s_t, a_t$**;

    - 在 **固定时间步 $t$** 下, 应用全期望公式:
        $$\begin{align}
            \_
            = \mathbb{E}_{\tau_{\le t}} \left\lbrack\ \mathbb{E}_{\tau_{> t} \mid \tau_{\le t}} \left\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \gamma^t G_t \mid \tau_{\le t} \ \right\rbrack \ \right\rbrack
        \end{align}$$
- **关键观察**:
    - 在给定到时刻 $t$ 时 (含 $t$), $\nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) \cdot \gamma^t$ 是一个确定性 **常数**, 因为 $s_t, a_t$ 已包含在 $\tau_{\le t}$ 中.
    - 因此可以将其提出内层期望, 即:
    $$\begin{align}
        \_
        = \mathbb{E}_{\tau_{\le t}} \left\lbrack\ \underline{\nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) \cdot \gamma^t} \cdot \mathbb{E}_{\tau_{> t} \mid \tau_{\le t}} \left\lbrack\ G_t \mid \tau_{\le t} \ \right\rbrack \ \right\rbrack
    \end{align}$$
    - 根据 RL 设定, **环境与策略均满足马尔可夫性**, 
    - 即 **$G_t$ 的分布与历史轨迹无关, 只依赖 $t$ 时刻的 $s_t, a_t$**, 得:
        $$\begin{align}
            \_
            &= \mathbb{E}_{\tau_{\le t}} \left\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) \cdot \gamma^t \cdot \underline{\mathbb{E}_{\tau_{> t} \mid s_t,a_t} \left\lbrack\ G_t \mid s_t, a_t \ \right\rbrack} \ \right\rbrack \\
            &= \mathbb{E}_{\tau_{\le t}} \left\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) \cdot \gamma^t \underline{Q^{\pi_\theta}(s_t, a_t)} \ \right\rbrack
        \end{align}$$
- **化简外层期望**:
    - 注意 $\tau_{\le t}$ 包含 $s_t,a_t$, 而 $Q^{\pi_\theta}(s_t,a_t)$ 只依赖于 $s_t,a_t$ (与 $\tau_{< t}$ 无关), 因此: 
        $$\begin{align}
            \_
            = \mathbb{E}_{\underline{(s_t,a_t) \sim p_\theta(\tau)}} \left\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) \cdot \gamma^t Q^{\pi_\theta}(s_t, a_t) \ \right\rbrack

    \end{align}$$
- **第一阶段推导完毕**.

### A 函数形式
> 推导目标:
> - 从 Q 函数形式出发:
>   $$\begin{align}\boxed{
>   \nabla_\theta J(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{s_t \sim d^{\pi_\theta}, a_t\sim\pi_\theta} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \underline{Q^{\pi_\theta}(s_t, a_t)} \ \Big\rbrack
>   }\end{align}$$
>   其中 $Q^{\pi_\theta}(s_t, a_t) = \mathbb{E}_{\tau_{> t}\mid s_t,a_t} \left\lbrack\ G_t \ |\ s_t, a_t \ \right\rbrack$. <br>
> - 得到策略梯度的 A 函数形式:
>   $$\begin{align}\boxed{
>   \nabla_\theta J(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{s_t \sim d^{\pi_\theta}, a_t\sim\pi_\theta} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \underline{A^{\pi_\theta}(s_t, a_t)} \ \Big\rbrack
>   }\end{align}$$
>   其中 $A^{\pi_\theta}(s_t, a_t) = Q^{\pi_\theta}(s_t, a_t) - V^{\pi_\theta}(s_t)$. <br>

- 根据 [基线不变性](#基线不变性), 状态价值函数 $V^{\pi_\theta}(s_t)$ **不依赖于当前动作 $a_t$**, **满足基线函数性质**;
- 因此:
    $$\begin{align}
        \mathbb{E}_{s_t \sim d^{\pi_\theta}, a_t\sim\pi_\theta} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \underline{V^{\pi_\theta}(s_t)} \ \Big\rbrack = 0
    \end{align}$$
- 代入, 得:
    $$\begin{align}
        \nabla_\theta J(\theta) 
        &= \mathbb{E}_{(s_t, a_t) \sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \underline{\Big( Q^{\pi_\theta}(s_t, a_t) - V^{\pi_\theta}(s_t) \Big)} \ \right\rbrack \\
        &= \mathbb{E}_{(s_t, a_t) \sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \underline{A^{\pi_\theta}(s_t, a_t)} \ \right\rbrack
    \end{align}$$
- **推导完毕**.

### Score Function 恒等式
<!--END_SECTION:keyword-->
> $$\mathbb{E}_{x \sim p_\theta(x)} \left\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta \log p_\theta(x) \ \right\rbrack = 0$$
<!-- > 即 **得分函数的期望为零**. -->

- 定义 **Score Function (得分函数)**:
    - 对于一个参数化的概率分布 $p_\theta(x)$, 其 **Score** 被定义为 **对数似然函数关于参数 $\theta$ 的梯度**:
    $$s(\theta) = \nabla_\theta \log p_\theta(x)$$
- **Score Function 恒等式** 表明 **得分函数的期望为零**.
- **证明**:
    $$\begin{aligned}
        \mathbb{E}_{x \sim p_\theta(x)} \left\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta \log p_\theta(x) \ \right\rbrack 
        &= \int p_\theta(x) \nabla_\theta \log p_\theta(x) \ dx \\
        &= \int \nabla_\theta p_\theta(x) \ dx \quad && \scriptstyle\text{// 对数微分性质}& \\
        &= \nabla_\theta \int p_\theta(x) \ dx \quad && \scriptstyle\text{// 正则条件下, 积分和求导可交换}& \\
        &= \nabla_\theta 1 = 0
    \end{aligned}$$
    > 正则条件: 1) 积分域与参数无关, 2) 函数关于参数可微,  3) 积分收敛良好
- **重要推论**:
    - 对于一个随机变量 $x \sim p_\theta(x)$ 和一个 (几乎处处) 可微函数 $f(x)$, 只要满足一定的正则条件, 使积分和求导可交换, 则以下等式成立 (利用 **对数微分性质** 即可证明, 过程略):
        $$\nabla_\theta \mathbb{E}_{x \sim p_\theta(x)} \left\lbrack f(x) \right\rbrack = \mathbb{E}_{x \sim p_\theta(x)} \left\lbrack\ f(x) \nabla_\theta \log p_\theta(x) \ \right\rbrack$$
        > 这个公式通常被称为 **REINFORCE 恒等式** 或 **Likelihood Ratio 恒等式**, 是 **策略梯度定理的基石**.
    - **直观理解**: 该恒等式将一个**期望的梯度**, 转化为了另一个**期望**;
        - 这意味着不需要知道 $f(x)$ 本身关于 $\theta$ 的梯度, 只需要知道概率分布 $p_\theta(x)$ 关于 $\theta$ 的梯度 (即 Score Function), 就可以估计出期望 $\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack\ f(x) \ \right\rbrack$ 的梯度; 
        - 这对于 $f(x)$ 不可微或者是一个黑盒函数的情况至关重要.

### 基线不变性
<!--END_SECTION:keyword-->
> 设 $b(\cdot)$ 是任意 **不依赖于当前动作 $a_t$** 的函数 (称为基线), 则:
> $$\boxed{\mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot b(\cdot) \ \right\rbrack = 0}$$
>>  
> **推论 1**:对于任意回报函数 $G(\tau)$ 和基线 $b(\cdot)$, 有:
> $$\mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot G(\tau) \ \right\rbrack = \mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \Big(G(\tau) - b(\cdot)\Big) \right\rbrack$$
> **推论 2**: 整条回报 $G(\tau)$ 可被替换为从时刻 $t$ 开始的折扣回报 $\gamma^t G_t$:
> $$\mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot G(\tau) \ \right\rbrack = \mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \gamma^t G_t \right\rbrack$$
>> 其中 $\displaystyle G_t = \sum_{k=t}^{\infty} \gamma^{k-t}\ R_{k+1}$

- 根据期望的线性性质, 下证对于 **每个固定的 $t$**:
    $$\mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot b(\cdot) \ \right\rbrack = 0$$
1. **轨迹分解**:
    - 定义:
        $$\tau = (h_t, a_t, f_t)$$
        其中: 
        - $h_t = (s_0,a_0,r_0,\dots,s_{t-1},a_{t-1},r_{t-1},s_t)$ : 到时刻 $t$ 的历史, **含 $s_t$**;
        - $a_t$ : 时刻 $t$ 的动作;
        - $f_t = (r_t,s_{t+1},a_{t+1},r_{t+1},\dots,s_T)$ : 未来轨迹.
    - 根据 **联合分布的链式法则**, 将轨迹概率分解为:
        $$p_\theta(\tau) = p(h_t)\ p(a_t|h_t)\ p(f_t|h_t,a_t)$$
    - 因为策略 $\pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)$ 服从马尔可夫假设, 动作 $a_t$ 只与状态 $s_t$ 有关, 即:
        $$p(a_t|h_t) = \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)$$
    - 因此:
        $$p_\theta(\tau) = p(h_t) \cdot \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) \cdot p(f_t|h_t,a_t)$$
2. **关键观察**:
    - 按定义, 基线 $b(\cdot)$ 只依赖于状态 $s_t$, 而 $s_t$ 完全由 $h_t$ 决定; 
    - 因此, **在给定 $h_t$ 条件下, $b(\cdot)$ 是常量**
3. **全期望公式展开**:
    $$\begin{aligned}
        &\mathbb{E}_{\tau=(h_t,a_t,f_t)} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\ b(\cdot) \Big\rbrack \\
        =\ \ & \mathbb{E}_{h_t} \bigg\lbrack\ \mathbb{E}_{a_t,f_t|h_t} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\ {\color{blue}b(\cdot)} \ \Big|\ h_t \Big\rbrack \bigg\rbrack &&\ \scriptstyle //\ 全期望公式(条件形式) \\
        =\ \ & \mathbb{E}_{h_t} \bigg\lbrack {\color{blue}b(\cdot)} \ \underline{\mathbb{E}_{a_t,f_t|h_t}} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) \ \Big|\ h_t \Big\rbrack \bigg\rbrack &&\ \scriptstyle //\ h_t\ 条件下,\ b(\cdot)\ 为常数 \\
        =\ \ & \mathbb{E}_{h_t} \bigg\lbrack {\color{blue}b(\cdot)} \ \underline{\mathbb{E}_{a_t|h_t}} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\ \Big|\ h_t \Big\rbrack \bigg\rbrack &&\ \scriptstyle //\ 被积函数与\ f_t\ 无关 (\textbf{未来轨迹的独立性})
    \end{aligned}$$
    > **未来轨迹的独立性**: $f_t$ 与 $\nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)$ 在给定 $h_t$ 条件下相互独立.
4. **内层期望分析**:
    $$\begin{aligned}
        \mathbb{E}_{a_t|h_t} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\ \Big|\ h_t \Big\rbrack 
        &= \sum_{a_t} \underline{p(a_t|h_t)} \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) \\
        &= \sum_{a_t} \underline{\pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)} \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) &&\quad \scriptstyle{//\ p(a_t|h_t) \ =\ \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)} \\
        &= \mathbb{E}_{a_t \sim \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)} \left\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) \right\rbrack = 0 &&\quad \scriptstyle\text{// Score Function 恒等式}
    \end{aligned}$$
    > [_Score Function 恒等式_](#score-function-恒等式)
5. **得出结论**:
    $$\begin{aligned}
        \mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim p_\theta(\tau)} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) \cdot b(\cdot) \Big\rbrack
        &= \mathbb{E}_{h_t} \bigg\lbrack {b(\cdot)} \cdot \underline{\mathbb{E}_{a_t|h_t} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\ \Big|\ h_t \Big\rbrack} \bigg\rbrack \\
        &= \mathbb{E}_{h_t} \bigg\lbrack b(\cdot) \cdot \underline{0} \bigg\rbrack = 0
    \end{aligned}$$
    > **证毕.**
6. **推论 1** 证明: 
    - 根据期望的线性性质, 易得:
    $$\begin{aligned}
        & \mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot \Big(G(\tau) - b(\cdot)\Big) \right\rbrack \\
        =\ \ & \mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot G(\tau) \right\rbrack - \underset{=0}{\underline{\mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot b(\cdot) \right\rbrack}} \\
        =\ \ & \mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack\ \sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot G(\tau) \right\rbrack
    \end{aligned}$$
    > **证毕.**
7. **推论 2**:
    - 等价于证明:
    $$\mathbb{E}_{\tau \sim p_\theta(\tau)} \left\lbrack \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \nabla_\theta \log \pi_\theta(a_t | s_t) \big(G_0 - \gamma^t G_t\big) \right\rbrack = 0$$
    - 根据 $G_t$ 定义, 以及 $G(\tau) = G_0$, 令
    $$b_t = G_0 - \gamma^t G_t = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \gamma^{k}\ R_{k+1} - \gamma^t \sum_{k=t}^{\infty} \gamma^{k-t}\ R_{k+1} = \sum_{k=0}^{t-1} \gamma^{k} R_{k+1}$$
    - $b_t$ 的依赖至 $t-1$ 时刻为止, 即 **不依赖当前动作 $a_t$**, 满足基线函数性质.
    > 证毕.

    - 因为策略 $\pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)$ 服从马尔可夫假设, 动作 $a_t$ 只与状态 $s_t$ 有关, 即:
        $$p(a_t|h_t) = \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)$$
    - 因此:
        $$p_\theta(\tau) = p(h_t) \cdot \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) \cdot p(f_t|h_t,a_t)$$
2. **关键观察**:
    - $G_0 - G_t = \displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{t-1} \gamma^{k} r_{k+1}$ 涉及的奖励 $(r_0,...,r_{t-1})$ 都已在 $h_t$ 中被确定;
    - 因此, **给定 $h_t$ 条件下, $G_0 - G_t$ 不再是随机变量, 而是一个常量**.
3. **应用全期望公式**:
    $$\begin{aligned}
        &\mathbb{E}_{\tau=(h_t,a_t,f_t)} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot (G_0 - G_t) \Big\rbrack \\
        =\ \ & \mathbb{E}_{h_t} \bigg\lbrack\ \mathbb{E}_{a_t,f_t|h_t} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot (G_0 - G_t) \ \Big|\ h_t \Big\rbrack \bigg\rbrack &&\ \scriptstyle\text{// 全期望公式} \\
        =\ \ & \mathbb{E}_{h_t} \bigg\lbrack (G_0 - G_t)\ \mathbb{E}_{a_t,f_t|h_t} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) \ \Big|\ h_t \Big\rbrack \bigg\rbrack &&\ \scriptstyle\text{// 提取常量因子} \\
        =\ \ & \mathbb{E}_{h_t} \bigg\lbrack (G_0 - G_t)\ \mathbb{E}_{a_t|h_t} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\ \Big|\ h_t \Big\rbrack \bigg\rbrack &&\ \scriptstyle\text{// 未来轨迹}\ f_t\ \text{的独立性} \\
    \end{aligned}$$

4. **内层期望分析**:
    $$\begin{aligned}
        & \mathbb{E}_{a_t|h_t} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\ \Big|\ h_t \Big\rbrack \\
        =\ \ & \sum_{a_t} \underline{p(a_t|h_t)} \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) \\
        =\ \ & \sum_{a_t} \underline{\pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)} \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) &&\quad \scriptstyle{//\ p(a_t|h_t) \ =\ \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)} \\
        =\ \ & \mathbb{E}_{a_t \sim \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)} \left\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t) \right\rbrack = 0 &&\quad \scriptstyle\text{// Score Function 恒等式}
    \end{aligned}$$

    $$\mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim p_\theta(\tau)} \Big\lbrack\ \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\cdot (G_0 - G_t) \Big\rbrack = \mathbb{E}_{h_t} \bigg\lbrack (G_0 - G_t)\cdot 0 \bigg\rbrack = 0$$
